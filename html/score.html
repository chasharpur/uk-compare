Alignment Scorer

This page lets you compare a computed alignment against a hand-edited reference alignment. Above the computed alignment panel on the left click Browse... to load a saved alignment from the Align Texts page. Next load another alignment from the test-data folder in the uk-compare GitHub repository, which you have downloaded to your computer, or any manually edited alignment of your own making. The reference alignment and the computed one must be of exactly the same work and compare exactly the same versions. Now click the Score button, and the percentage of the similarity between the two alignments will be displayed in the box next to the Score button.
