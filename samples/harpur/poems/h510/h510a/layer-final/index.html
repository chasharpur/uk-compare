X.

Yet fear moved not Idomenëus—such As might have siezed upon some tender boy. But firmly stood he, like a mountain boar, That, confident of his own prowess, dares Await the mighty din of many men Coming against him, out in the wild pastures: He bristles up his back; his red eyes glare With fiery anger, and he whets his tusks: Even so the spear-renowned Idomnëus, Awaited the forthcoming there against him Of swift Æneas, nor retired.
