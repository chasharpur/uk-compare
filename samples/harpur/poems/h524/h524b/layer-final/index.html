A Pleasant Song

Songs, Odes, and other Lyrics.

X Song.

Oh, Love it is a pleasant song That wakens in the Spring, When days are waxing bright and long And bowers are blossoming; To strengthen all the powers that bless, And gladden all employ, To bring a joy to tenderness, And tenderness to joy.
In the warm influence of the skies It anthems Beauty’s birth, Or raineth thence to fertilize The bridals of the earth: And then, if measureful and strong It cometh to our door, Oh, Love it is a pleasant song To cheer us evermore.
Hark! through the bursting of the buds, And through the breezes’ mirth, And through the light,—in golden floods It warbles o’er the earth: But have we done its spirit wrong, Or dared to hold it vain? Then Love it is a pleasant song We never hear again!
