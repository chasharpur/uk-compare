Paraphrase of the VIII Psalm

O Lord how excellent is thy name! Let all the Earth adore the same: Thy glory higher than heaven aspires, And burns through all her starry fires.

Out of the mouths of babes, o Lord, Thou givest the spirit of thy word To lighten with such power and will, That, lo! even the avenger’s still.

When I consider this thy sky, Stretched out so far and built so high; The mild Moon’s silver visage fair And all the golden glories there:

Oh, what is Man, that he should be A child of such regard to thee? And what the son of Man, that thou Should’st set dominion on his brow

6

For only than the Angels, he A little lower was made to be; When thou didst crown him at his birth, Thy representative on Earth.

And therefore is he Lord of all Her living things, both great and small, That walk the ground or wing the air, Or through the paths of Ocean fare.

How excellent is then thy name? Let all mankind adore thy same! Thy glory higher than heaven aspires, And burns through all her starry fires.
